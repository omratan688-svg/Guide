ONLINE TOOLS GUIDE

6 Free Online Tools for Sellers, Freelancers & Creators

From calculating payment and marketplace fees to estimating freelance earnings and generating better Twitch titles, these six practical tools can make everyday online work faster and easier.

By Omratan • Updated August 9, 2026 • Online Tools
Quick overview: If you sell products online, accept digital payments, freelance on marketplaces, or create content, repetitive calculations and brainstorming can take unnecessary time. These tools are designed to simplify those tasks.

Making money online involves more than simply listing a product or publishing a service. Sellers need to understand costs, freelancers need to estimate their actual earnings, and creators need fresh ideas for their content.

Manually calculating fees for every transaction or coming up with a new title every time you stream can become repetitive. Specialized online tools can make these everyday tasks much faster.

Here are six practical tools for different parts of the online selling, freelancing, payment, and creator economy.

1

Stripe Fee Calculator

Online businesses, freelancers, and creators often accept payments through payment processors. The amount a customer pays may not be identical to the amount received after applicable processing costs.

The Stripe Fee Calculator helps you estimate potential payment processing costs and understand the amount that may remain from a transaction.

It can be useful when you're pricing a service, sending an invoice, checking a transaction, or comparing different payment amounts.

Who can use it?

  • Freelancers accepting payments
  • Small online businesses
  • SaaS businesses
  • Digital product sellers
  • Online service providers
  • Creators selling products or services

Instead of estimating the deduction manually, you can quickly check a transaction and use the result as part of your pricing calculations.

2

Vinted Fee Calculator

Reselling second-hand clothing can be a simple way to turn unused items or sourced inventory into extra income. However, the listed selling price isn't necessarily the same as your final profit.

The Vinted Fee Calculator helps sellers estimate the numbers associated with a Vinted sale and think more clearly about potential margins.

This is particularly useful when you're deciding whether an item is worth sourcing for resale.

Estimated profit = Selling price − purchase cost − applicable costs

Useful for resellers

  • Clothing sellers
  • Vintage resellers
  • Second-hand sellers
  • Casual Vinted sellers
  • People starting a resale business

Checking the numbers before buying inventory can help you avoid products with margins that are too small to justify the effort.

3

Venmo Fee Calculator

Payment costs can vary depending on the type of transaction and the circumstances of the payment. Checking the potential cost before a transaction can make pricing and payout estimates easier.
